Investment Philosophy

I invest in companies at inflection points — where improving free cash flow, a clear catalyst, and market mispricing converge. My process is bottom-up and scenario-driven, prioritizing downside protection before underwriting upside.

01

FCF Quality

Earnings mean little without cash. I prioritize durable and improving free cash flow profiles.

02

Catalyst Identification

Mispricing alone is not enough. I look for events that can close the valuation gap.

03

Downside First

Bear-case valuation anchors each model. The goal is asymmetric risk/reward, not just headline upside.
