Investment Philosophy

I seek companies at inflection points — where improving free cash flow, a clear catalyst, and market mispricing converge. My process is bottom-up and scenario-driven, always stress-testing the downside before sizing the upside.

01

FCF Quality

Earnings mean nothing without cash. I prioritize companies with durable, improving free cash flow profiles.

02

Catalyst Identification

Mispricing alone isn't enough. I look for near-term events that can close the gap — earnings revisions, contract wins, regulatory shifts.

03

Downside First

Bear-case scenario valuation anchors every model. Asymmetric risk/reward is the target — not just absolute upside.
